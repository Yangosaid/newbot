Renovation guide

How to prepare and compare a quote

What information to share, which items to check and how to tell the cost of materials apart from the scope of work.

Read the guide
  1. Describe the scope room by room. Say what changes and what stays in each space.
  2. Share photos and rough measurements. They avoid assumptions and make the proposal more accurate.
  3. Check each line item. Demolition, installations, surfaces, joinery, painting and debris removal.
  4. Separate materials from labour. That way you can change a finish without losing sight of the rest of the quote.
  5. Compare like with like. Two prices are only comparable if they include the same work and quality.
  6. Ask about scheduling. Expected dates, the order of works and whether you can stay in the property.
What renovations do you do in Girona?

Full and room-by-room renovations: kitchens, bathrooms, painting and finishes, flooring, drywall, electrical and plumbing work. We can take care of a single room or your entire home.

How much does a renovation in Girona cost?

It depends on the scope, the condition of the property, the materials and the schedule. That is why we prepare each quote from your project, with the included work itemised, rather than giving generic prices.

What do I need to request a quote?

Just tell us what you want to renovate and which town the property is in. A few photos and rough measurements help us understand the space from the start.

Can I renovate just the bathroom or the kitchen?

Yes. We do partial, single-room renovations as well as full ones. Tell us what you want to change and what you would like to keep.

Does sending a request commit me to anything?

No. Sending a request is just the first step for us to learn about your project. There is no commitment: you decide after reviewing the proposal.
