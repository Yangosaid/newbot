Data controller

Y&M REFORNOVA SOCIEDAD LIMITADA, with the contact details shown above, is responsible for the personal data you provide.

What data we process

Only what you choose to send us when you contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email: your name, contact details, town and information about your project, including any photos you share.

The quote form on this website does not send data to any server: it prepares the message on your device and you decide whether to send it. If you save a draft, it is stored only in your browser and you can delete it at any time.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, prepare your quote and, if you go ahead, manage the renovation. The legal basis is your request and, where applicable, the contract.

How long we keep it

For as long as needed to handle your request and, if there is a contract, for the periods required by law.

Who we share it with

We do not share your data with third parties unless required by law. WhatsApp and your email provider process messages under their own policies.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction or portability of your data by writing to reformamacro@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. It only stores the form draft in your browser when you ask it to.
